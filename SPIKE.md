**2026-10-01, built the day the charter was ratified**, as section 10 of `haro/CHARTER.md`
asked, under `haro/spike/`. Every number below comes from `python haro/spike/haro_forge.py`
(52 s, six stages, `PASS`), run on this machine on 2026-10-01; the full transcript of the
run is kept beside the oracle artefacts in `runtime/haro-forge-oracle/full_run.txt` (not
committed). The guard was made to fail before it was trusted: `--plant` flips a verdict in

**the third, stzu v0.3, the same night** -- the guard's default declaration is v0.3's since
then, 62.6 s a pass, `full_run_v03.txt` its transcript, and stage 3 is pinned at a floor.
**The fourth, stzu v0.4, the next day**: the fleet language declared over the machine

## The six stages, measured

**1. The declaring altitude runs on `rnx`.** The Ring meta-court passes the 28 pinned stzu
fixtures (8 accepts with structural expectations, 20 rejects with their refusal fragment)
on `rnxc` and on the oracle, and the two runtimes print the same bytes on every fixture.
The Luau meta-court passes the same 28. `machine.stzu` reads as 7 declarations, 5 forms, 0
expressions, 3 refusals on both, the meta-court's own count.

**2. The forge is A/B'd too.** The court and the grammar forged from `machine.stzu` are
byte-identical whether the forge runs on `rnxc` or on the oracle (1,724 and 2,395 bytes).

**3. The forged court against `harb`, 120 machine fixtures (26 accepts, 94 rejects).**

The 70 "beyond" are the measurement the charter asked for: the rules a court carries that
its declaration does not -- a profile's kernel, a board's consoles, a menu of words, a
default, the order of services, a capability lattice, a reference that must resolve. They
are the `WORLD`, `REFINE` and rule seats the charter's section 3 names as the growth of the
declaring altitude, and this is their size on a real language: **70 of 120 fixtures, 94 of
the court's 1,443 lines of Zig**. The accept expectations the declaration cannot claim say
the same from the other side: `board`, `console`, `libc` (defaults), `order` (AFTER),
`granted` (the lattice).

## What it found, each a numbered finding in `haro/FINDINGS.md`

- **HF-30** Ring's ordering operators are numeric only: `"a" < "b"` raises R41 on the oracle,
  and `rnxc` orders strings and answers 1. The port's first run on the oracle found it; a
  Ring++ divergence to decide, not a defect of the port.
- **HF-31** `machine.stzu` lags `machine.zig`: 13 clauses, the `PEER` kind and the fleet
  kinds; `makeen_box.machine` is not a sentence of its own language's declaration.
- **HF-32** stzu v0.1 has no union type; `ADDRESS` (`dhcp` or a string) and `EGRESS`
  (`none` or a list) cannot be declared as the court reads them.
- **HF-33** The forge's first version lost its output directory on the oracle because a
  helper assigned `cOut`, the name of the top level's variable: Ring's own rule, that a
  function's bare assignment writes the global (analysis 2.7), reproduced in the spike's
  own code and caught by the A/B. `rnxc`, under Rule A, ran it correctly.
- **HF-34** The charter's section 10 said 54 fixtures; the file has 120. The number was
  07-SYSTEM's of 2026-09-12 and had grown.
- **HF-35** Two implementations of C6 3.2 fold a string's continuation differently: the Luau
  meta-court strips the whitespace before the newline as well, `harb` keeps it. No fixture
  exercises the difference.

**THE GUARD HAD A BLIND SPOT, AND THE RUNG WALKED INTO IT.** The first v0.3 court read
"67 agree, 47 beyond", and every one of the 47 carried harb's correct refusal -- a
constraint-driven one. The court's constraints had stopped firing: the rewrite of the
constraint loop (to skip a condition that reads through a dangling reference) inverted the
rule for a `WHEN` with no `THEN`, so a forbidden state was never refused. The guard
classifies "harb refuses, the court accepts" as *beyond the declaration* -- the class that
MEASURES -- and has no row for "the court got weaker", so a court that lost half its
rules read as a declaration that could not say them (HF-39). `AGREE_FLOOR` pins stage 3
on the default declaration at 117 now, made to fail against 200 before it was trusted.

with the constraint loop fixed they agree because `hosted_boards` is declared before
`rpi4_is_aarch64`, and the declared order is the court's order.

## The fourth rung -- stzu v0.4, a language over a language -- 2026-10-02

**Decided on the author's behalf** ("decide on what is next on my behalf"), among the
three he had been offered. Not the forge as an `rnxc` verb: that is `src/`, which the
brief keeps out of this session, so it is a request to the ringpp session. Not the
Softanza-resident timing alone: a measurement with no design question in it. The fleet
language, because the charter's section 3 says composition is the normal way to declare a
language, one language is one data point, and harobanda's fleet file is the estate's
second declared language with a native court and 32 fixtures -- every one of whose
members names a machine FILE the fleet court reads, judges with the machine court, and
reads through for the rules no single machine can fail. It is also what the three fixtures
left by v0.3 were, and it is inside `haro/`.

**What a language over a language needs**, read off the 32 fixtures and written as 23
v0.4 fixtures first (`spike/stzu4/`, the prose after): a `SENTENCE` seat type, whose value
names a file of another declared language, read beside the file being judged and judged
by that language's own court, with the words for a file that cannot be read and for one
its court refuses; a condition whose places are PATHS (`declaration.box`, the member's one
MACHINE; `server.declaration.peer`, the server's peers); a `VIEW`, a derived place by a
closed rule (the member's network on the fleet's link; the first member that serves it;
the peer the server promises this member an address as); quantifiers over a path and over
the `earlier` declarations of a kind; a place on the right of `=`; `self is VIEW`; a FORMAT
that `FOLD`s case; `MESSAGES` for a required seat absent; two shapes. The forge emits ONE
court carrying both languages' records; the court judges a member file by switching to
the machine record and back.

**Three things the fixtures caught that the design had wrong, each in a day's time:**

  A LIST OF REFERENCES WAS A SET, AND THE SET WAS EMPTY. The new path walker resolved
      `sees: [mount]` to the mounts it could find, so `sees present` was false when the
      mount was missing and `sees distinct` was false whenever a target did not
      resolve: five machine ACCEPTS refused, "is already named in display's SEES"
      with an empty `{other}`. The floor fired (98 against 117) and the stage-3 row
      said which five. `present`, `unique`, `distinct`, `empty` and `contains` read
      the clause AS WRITTEN now, resolved or not.
  A SEAT AND A KIND SHARED A NAME. A RETIREMENT's seat `member` is the kind `member`'s
      name, so `some member where ...` read the seat -- one declaration, not a set --
      and the forge refused the fleet declaration. A quantifier wants a set, so one
      name that is both is the kind to a quantifier and the seat to a path (HF-42).
  A BARE WORD ON THE RIGHT OF `=` IS A VALUE. `member.key = key` compared a member's
      key with the four-letter word "key", so a retirement of a key its member still
      held was refused for the next reason down the list (FR23). `member.key =
      self.key` says it; the rule was v0.3's and the fixture is what made it bite.

**And a rule had to be taken back the same hour it was made.** Inside a quantifier over
PEERs of the machine language, the fleet's `fleet.link` was out of reach, so the first fix
let a name the quantified declaration lacks fall back to the ON declaration's -- and v0.3's
R16 ("a bare seat inside a quantifier is the quantified declaration's; a seat it lacks is
refused") failed at once, on both runtimes. The fallback is for KINDS and whole-sentence
VIEWS of the outer language only; a seat of the ON declaration is `this.SEAT`, as before.
The fixture that was in the way was right.

## What the spike does not claim

- The forged court judges what the declaration says and nothing more; 70 of 120 fixtures
  need rules it cannot carry under v0.1, 25 under v0.2 (14 computations, 9 lags, 2
  fragments), and 3 under v0.3 (the fleet file's kinds, another language's), which the
  fourth rung declares as the fleet language itself. That is the measurement, not a
  shortfall of the forge.
- The fleet's acts are not its grammar: the roll, `verify` under a member's key and the
  fingerprint are harb's and stay so. The court of the composition judges the set.
- The five relations are a closed menu grown by five lines of the court, not the governed
  door to the computing altitude; a computation outside them is unsayable, by design.
- The refinement gate's trust floor was one rule (`trusted` for every point) under v0.1; v0.2
  declares a floor per point (`REFINE ... FLOOR`), and the guard's stage 5 measures it under
  `--stzu=machine_v02.stzu`.
- The grammar is sampled, not proven: 200 derivations, a seeded generator over the forge's
  own dialect.
- The timing is one machine, one file, minima of 15; the Softanza-resident case the
  charter's section 2.6 asks for is not this measurement, because a forged court loads no
  library.
