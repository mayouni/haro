~~~ The fixtures of rung 19 (Haro v0.19): node identity on the program graph of rung 18, for the Ring++ face.
~~~
~~~ Hand-composed BEFORE the matcher existed (the W discipline: fixtures first, code second). Each one is an edit: the Ring program as it
~~~ was (`--- old`), as it became (`--- new`), and what a matcher must answer.
~~~
~~~ THE NUMBERING, which the expected answers are written in. The old program's graph is numbered 1, 2, 3... in PRE-ORDER over its items:
~~~ an item (a statement, a definition, a comment, a parameter), then the items of its blocks in field order, and a block is a body
~~~ (`func` holds its parameters first, then its statements; `if` its `then`, its `elif`s, its `else`). An expression holds no id, except
~~~ the statements inside a lambda or a brace in it, which are items of that block.
~~~
~~~ `--- ids`   the NEW program's items in the same pre-order, each as the id it kept from the old one, or `*` for an item the edit made
~~~ `--- lost`  the old ids the new program does not hold, or `-` for none
~~~
~~~ The rules every row below pins (v19/IDENTITY.md): a definition is matched by its name; a statement by the hash of its whole text; what
~~~ is left in the same gap by a SIMILARITY of 0.6 on its tokens (an edit keeps its id, a replacement does not); a move inside one list or
~~~ one function by identical text; a renamed definition by the share of its body that is unchanged.
