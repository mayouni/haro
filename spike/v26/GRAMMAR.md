## What building it found, recorded (HF-130)

1. **No seat of Z is personal.** The constitution's `PERSONAL` names seats of the language's kinds,
   and in a language that declares schemas the seats hold shapes: `deposit`'s `member: text` is a
   field of an entity, a value in the sentence, not a seat Haro can name. The weaves are declared and
   judge nothing at this altitude, which is said in the declaration's RATIONALE, not hidden; the
   datum is governed where it lives -- the record plane, ZC4's stzlib and HaroBase slices. Whether
   Z's `field` form should carry a tag (`member: text` marked personal, a change to zing's grammar,
   HARO-Z-02's proposal) is a question for the author, not a rung taken here.
2. **The fixture was wrong once, the court right.** T8 and T10 were written citing a constraint by
   its name (`rule:a_formula_names_its_field`), the form a probe on the uncoded Z had shown; with a
   `CODE` declared the court cites the code (`rule:NO_COMPUTE`), the rule since v0.11, and T5 and T7
   already expected that form of a seat. Corrected after the court spoke, and said so in the case
   file's comment -- the opposite of HF-128's first finding, where the court moved to the fixture.
3. **The constitution found a question the world had not answered, and only one.** Declaring the
   tontine under all six articles changed its sentence by one clause (who holds the zone) and its
   language by what the articles name; the runtime, the cases and the questions did not move.
4. **TELLS and SAYS live together.** v0.16's rule -- a voice says, tells, or both -- carried Art. 3
   without a change: the voice that told the question gained the kinds it had never said.

## Refused here

- A grammar change for the rung's sake: none was needed, none was made.
- A personal seat invented to give the weaves something to judge (a member's contact on the zone, a
  treasurer's name): a sector's world carries no person, and a seat added for a court's sake is a
  lie the court would then enforce.
- A second adaptation of zing's fixture source beyond the holder: `tontine.z` is zing's text in the
  Commons' shape plus one clause, and `tontine/tontine_unheld.z` keeps the original beside it.
